# Product

Detailed project handoff: [PRODUCT_SPEC.md](PRODUCT_SPEC.md).

## Register

product

## Users

Students in grades 9–12 in Kazakhstan exploring competitions, programs, scholarships, and other opportunities. They need to find relevant options and understand deadlines, eligibility, and cost quickly.

## Product Purpose

A searchable, multilingual catalog of verified opportunities. The main task is to narrow the list and open an official source for an opportunity worth pursuing.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, direct, and energetic. The visual direction follows [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Anti-references

Formal education brochures, generic corporate stock imagery, crowded dashboards, and unsupported claims of outcomes.

## Design Principles

- Make search and filtering immediate.
- Give each opportunity a clear title, deadline, and next action.
- Show verified facts and measurable proof where the catalog has them.
- Keep dense information readable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard access, reduced motion, and full support for Russian, Kazakh, and English.
